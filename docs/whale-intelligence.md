# Whale Intelligence

## Intelligence philosophy

Whale Intelligence transforms classified canonical wallets into explainable intelligence profiles. It does not track wallets, call providers, parse transactions, or infer identity with AI. Every category and score is derived from explicit classifications and supplied metrics.

## Scoring dimensions

Capital, behavior, influence, network, historical performance, cross-chain activity, and confidence are represented by independent immutable score models. They are deliberately not combined into one fixed score. `ScoringStrategy` allows future policies to evaluate the same dimensions without changing the profile contract.

## Explainability

Each profile retains wallet references, classifications, categories, raw dimension metrics, independent scores, confidence, relationships, and supporting evidence. Runtime translation preserves evidence references in canonical findings, assessments, and signals.

## Runtime execution

`WalletIntelligenceVerticalSlice` orchestrates Provider, Adapter, canonical Wallet, Wallet Discovery, Wallet Classification, Whale Intelligence, compiler, Knowledge Graph, correlation, reasoning, automation, distribution, and console summary. It reuses the existing synchronous Runtime without modification.

## Future tracking

Future tracking may submit canonical wallet observations and historical metrics to this engine. Provider polling, indexing, address monitoring, entity resolution, and alert policies remain separate components.

## Future real-time monitoring

Streaming systems can calculate approved metrics and invoke the same deterministic intelligence path. Durable queues, replay, checkpointing, deduplication, and external notifications belong outside the Whale Intelligence model and scoring contracts.
