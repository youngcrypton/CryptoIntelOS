# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Participation must remain free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while evaluating claims through evidence.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted personal attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or security processes.
- Retaliation against good-faith reporters or reviewers.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed proportionally.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and project-affiliated events or communications.
